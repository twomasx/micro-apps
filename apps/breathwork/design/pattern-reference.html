Breathwork: three design directions

Each one is the main breathing screen at 9:16, running the real timing for each technique. Pick a technique and tap start. On Android you'll also feel a light buzz at each change.

Tide

The screen fills with water as you breathe in and drains as you breathe out. You can follow it from the corner of your eye, even with your eyes half closed. The text turns white where the water covers it.

Pattern

Every technique has its own shape. Box is a square, 4-7-8 is a triangle with sides of 4, 7 and 8, and the cyclic sigh is two short climbs and one long slide. A dot traces it, so you learn the pattern by watching it once.

Typeform

No picture at all. The word is the guide: it stretches wide and gets bolder as you breathe in, holds, then narrows as you breathe out. It runs on one variable font, so it is tiny to load.

Timing: cyclic sigh 2 s in, 1 s top-up in, 6 s out. Box 4 in, 4 hold, 4 out, 4 hold. 4-7-8 is 4 in, 7 hold, 8 out. The research notes are placeholders until the spec is checked.
