# Thinking and Feeling

## What this scale measures

This scale measures what you put first when you decide and when you deal with people: logic and frankness, or sympathy and cooperation.

## Leaning T

Your answers leaned toward Thinking. You are likely to look at the logic of a problem first, to give honest criticism and to be comfortable disagreeing. It does not mean you have no feelings or do not care about people. It describes what you reach for first.

## Leaning F

Your answers leaned toward Feeling. You are likely to notice how others feel, to weigh the effect on people when you decide and to prefer cooperation over competition. It does not mean you cannot reason. It describes what you reach for first.

## Near the middle

You probably weigh logic and people about equally, or switch depending on what is at stake.

## Big Five

This scale uses questions written for the Big Five trait Agreeableness, plus four questions we wrote ourselves for the Thinking side. A result toward F matches higher Agreeableness. A result toward T matches lower Agreeableness. Agreeableness lines up with this scale less closely than Extraversion and Openness line up with theirs, so read this result as a rougher guide than E/I or S/N.
