# Sensing and Intuition

## What this scale measures

This scale measures what kind of information you are drawn to: facts, details and known methods, or ideas, patterns and imagination.

## Leaning S

Your answers leaned toward Sensing. You are likely to trust what you can see, measure and test, to prefer methods you know, and to have limited patience for abstract talk. It describes what you prefer to pay attention to. It says nothing about how well you think.

## Leaning N

Your answers leaned toward Intuition. You are likely to enjoy ideas for their own sake, to daydream, and to look for the pattern behind the facts. It describes what you prefer to pay attention to. It says nothing about how well you think.

## Near the middle

You probably move between the practical and the abstract depending on the task.

## Big Five

This scale uses questions written for the Big Five trait Openness to experience, mostly its imagination, intellect and adventurousness parts. A result toward N matches higher Openness. A result toward S matches lower Openness. Lower Openness means a preference for the familiar and the concrete. It is not a measure of intelligence.
