# Extraversion and Introversion

## What this scale measures

This scale measures where you get your energy and how much you seek out company, talk and attention.

## Leaning E

Your answers leaned toward Extraversion. You are likely to feel more awake around people, to start conversations and to be at ease with attention on you. A long quiet day alone is more likely to leave you flat than rested.

## Leaning I

Your answers leaned toward Introversion. You are likely to recover your energy in quiet, to say less in groups and to prefer staying out of the spotlight. It describes where your energy goes. It does not mean you dislike people or lack social skill.

## Near the middle

You probably act either way depending on the day, the people and how tired you are.

## Big Five

This scale uses questions written for the Big Five trait Extraversion. A result toward E matches higher Extraversion. A result toward I matches lower Extraversion. Researchers describe Extraversion as a continuous trait, with most people near the middle and fewer at either end.
