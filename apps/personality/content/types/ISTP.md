# ISTP

## Summary

You prefer quiet, concrete facts, decisions made by logic, and open options. You tend to learn how something works by handling it, and you trust what you have seen for yourself. You are likely to stay calm and practical when a problem turns up, and to fix it without much talk. You decide late, when the facts are in front of you. Schedules and long plans tend to feel like a cost. You usually keep your thoughts to yourself until someone asks.

## Strengths

You tend to stay level when something breaks and to go straight to what can be done. You notice how the parts of a thing fit together. You adapt quickly, because you were not attached to a plan to begin with. You usually say only what is needed, and what you say is accurate.

## Blind spots

You may leave decisions and paperwork until they become urgent. You can miss that someone wanted a response from you and not a fix. Long-range consequences may get less attention than the problem in front of you. You may go quiet in a conflict and leave the other person guessing.

## At work

You tend to want hands-on problems, room to work your own way and little supervision. You are likely to prefer doing the task over talking about it. Fixed routines, long meetings and rules that slow down an obvious fix tend to wear on you. You usually work in bursts, hardest when something is at stake.

## Under stress

You may withdraw and become blunt, or drop the task and go do something physical. You might put off the decision even longer. Space, a practical problem to solve and no pressure to explain how you feel usually help most.

## How others may read you

People may see you as calm, private and hard to read. Your short answers can come across as lack of interest. Those close to you tend to learn that you show up when something real needs doing. You may need to say what you are thinking a little sooner than feels natural.
