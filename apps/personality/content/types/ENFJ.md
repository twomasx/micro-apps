# ENFJ

## Summary

You prefer company and activity, ideas and possibilities, decisions that consider people, and a clear plan. You tend to see what a person or group could become and to organize people toward it. You are likely to talk easily with most people and to pick up how they feel. You like to turn concern for others into a plan with steps and dates. Disharmony in a group tends to bother you, and you usually move to settle it.

## Strengths

You tend to bring out effort in other people by showing that you believe in them. You explain an idea in a way that makes people want to take part. You keep a group pointed at its goal and attend to how its members are doing. You usually follow through on what you organize.

## Blind spots

You may decide what is good for someone without asking them. You can take on too many people's needs and neglect your own. Hard facts that spoil a hopeful picture may get less attention than they should. You may take disagreement as a sign that a relationship is in trouble.

## At work

You tend to want a shared purpose, people to guide or teach and a clear plan. You are likely to prefer working with a group over working alone. Cynicism, unresolved conflict and work that helps no one tend to drain you. You usually end up in the middle of a team's communication.

## Under stress

You may overcommit, then become controlling or easily hurt. You might start picking apart your own reasoning and other people's, which is unlike you. Time alone to sort out what is yours to carry, then an honest talk, usually helps.

## How others may read you

People may see you as warm, persuasive and sure of the direction. Some may feel managed. Those close to you tend to feel strongly supported. You may need to let people reach their own conclusions, even when you can see a faster route.
