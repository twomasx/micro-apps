# ESFJ

## Summary

You prefer company and activity, concrete facts, decisions that consider people, and a clear plan. You tend to keep track of the people around you: who needs what, who has not been heard from, what was promised. You are likely to organize things so that others are looked after, and to follow through. You value cooperation and established ways of doing things. Conflict and loose ends both tend to bother you. People usually find you warm and easy to approach.

## Strengths

You tend to bring people together and make sure the practical side is handled. You remember birthdays, preferences and who is going through a hard time. You do what you said you would. You usually notice tension in a group early and act to ease it.

## Blind spots

You may take on other people's problems until you are overloaded. You can avoid a needed disagreement to keep things pleasant. A break from tradition may feel wrong to you before you have weighed it. You may feel hurt when your effort goes unthanked, without saying so.

## At work

You tend to want a cooperative team, clear expectations and contact with people through the day. You are likely to prefer agreed routines over constant change. Cold or competitive settings, and criticism given without care, tend to wear on you. You usually keep the team's practical and social life running.

## Under stress

You may try harder to please everyone and grow resentful when it does not work. You might become unusually critical or start to doubt your own judgment. Talking with someone who appreciates you, and dropping one obligation, usually help.

## How others may read you

People may see you as friendly, organized and generous with your time. Some may find your help more than they asked for. Those close to you tend to feel well looked after. You may need to ask what someone wants before you arrange it for them.
