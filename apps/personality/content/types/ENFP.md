# ENFP

## Summary

You prefer company and activity, ideas and possibilities, decisions guided by your values, and open options. You tend to get excited by new ideas and to share them out loud with whoever is near. You are likely to connect with people fast and to care how they feel. You see many ways a thing could go and may dislike picking just one. Routine and detailed follow-through tend to hold less interest for you than starting something new.

## Strengths

You tend to see possibilities in people and situations that others have written off. You bring energy to a new idea and get others interested in it. You make connections between things that seem unrelated. You usually make people feel heard, and they open up to you quickly.

## Blind spots

You may start more than you can finish. You can say yes to too many people because each request seemed worth it at the time. Details, deadlines and money matters may get left late. You may avoid giving bad news and let a problem run on.

## At work

You tend to want variety, people to think out loud with and freedom to try things. You are likely to prefer starting projects over maintaining them. Strict routines, close monitoring and working alone for long periods tend to drain you. You usually do your best work when you believe in the purpose.

## Under stress

You may scatter your effort across too many things and finish none. You might fix on one small detail or worry, which is unlike you. Talking it through with someone, then cutting the list down to one or two things, usually helps.

## How others may read you

People may see you as enthusiastic, warm and full of ideas. Some may doubt you will follow through. Those close to you tend to know your interest in them is real and lasting. You may need to under-promise, so that what you deliver matches what you said.
