# INFJ

## Summary

You prefer quiet, ideas and patterns, decisions that weigh the effect on people, and a clear plan. You tend to think for a long time about what things mean and where they are heading, mostly in private. You care how choices affect others, and you like to act on that with a plan instead of leaving it open. You are likely to have a few close relationships more than many light ones. You may hold strong convictions that few people hear about.

## Strengths

You tend to see where a situation between people is heading before it is said out loud. You connect an idea to what it would mean for the people involved. When you care about a goal, you organize yourself and keep at it. You usually listen closely and remember what was underneath the words.

## Blind spots

You may form a picture of how things should go and hold to it after the facts have moved. You can take in other people's feelings until you have little energy left. Practical details may get less attention than the meaning behind them. You may expect others to sense what you have not told them.

## At work

You tend to want work that fits your values, room to think alone and a clear end point. You are likely to prefer depth on one thing over switching between many. Conflict in a team tends to weigh on you more than it does on others. You usually do your best thinking in writing or after time to reflect.

## Under stress

You may withdraw, go silent and turn the problem over alone for too long. You might become unusually strict about plans, or suddenly cut off something that has drained you. Time alone, followed by one honest conversation, usually helps more than being cheered up.

## How others may read you

People may find you warm but hard to know. You may seem calm while a lot is going on inside. Some are surprised by how firm you are once a value of yours is crossed. You may need to share your thinking earlier, because your conclusions can seem to come from nowhere.
