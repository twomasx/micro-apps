# ENTP

## Summary

You prefer company and activity, ideas and patterns, decisions made by logic, and open options. You tend to think out loud and to test ideas by arguing them with other people. You are likely to question the usual way of doing things and to enjoy a debate even when you do not hold a firm side. You see many possibilities and may resist closing any of them. Routine, schedules and finishing details tend to hold less interest for you than the next problem.

## Strengths

You tend to come up with several ways around a problem while others are still describing it. You spot the weak point in an argument quickly. You are at ease changing course when a better idea appears. You usually enjoy being challenged, so people can disagree with you openly.

## Blind spots

You may argue a point for the exercise and leave the other person feeling attacked. You can lose interest once the hard part is solved and the routine part begins. Commitments and deadlines may slip. You may handle a personal matter as a puzzle when the other person wanted sympathy.

## At work

You tend to want new problems, quick-thinking people and freedom from fixed procedure. You are likely to prefer designing the approach over carrying out every step. Repetition, slow decisions and rules kept only out of habit tend to frustrate you. You usually have more ideas than time.

## Under stress

You may jump between tasks, argue more and finish less. You might fix on small details or on how your body feels, which is unlike you. Rest, exercise and choosing one thing to complete usually help.

## How others may read you

People may see you as quick, funny and willing to say what others will not. Some may find you tiring or think you argue for the sake of it. Those who know you tend to value that you take their ideas seriously enough to test them. You may need to say when you agree, not only when you object.
