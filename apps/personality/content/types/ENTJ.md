# ENTJ

## Summary

You prefer company and activity, ideas and patterns, decisions made by logic, and a clear plan. You tend to see where a group could go and to start directing it there, out loud. You are likely to set goals, lay out the steps and push for a decision. You say what you think of an idea directly, and you expect others to do the same. Inefficiency and indecision tend to frustrate you. You usually have more energy for a challenge than for keeping things as they are.

## Strengths

You tend to see the long-range goal and the structure needed to reach it. You make decisions and accept responsibility for them. You speak up when something is not working. You usually keep pushing through obstacles that would stop a plan held less firmly.

## Blind spots

You may move ahead before others have had time to think or object. You can dismiss a concern because it was raised with feeling instead of argument. Details of how the work gets done day to day may escape you. You may count rest and patience as lost time.

## At work

You tend to want a large goal, authority to organize toward it and capable people. You are likely to prefer a fast decision that can be corrected over a slow one. Unclear direction, repeated mistakes and people who will not say what they think tend to frustrate you. You usually take the lead when no one else does.

## Under stress

You may push yourself and others harder and listen less. You might feel suddenly alone or unappreciated, which is unlike you. Stepping back, asking one trusted person for a frank view and taking real time off usually help.

## How others may read you

People may see you as decisive, confident and demanding. Some may find you intimidating or impatient. Those who work with you tend to respect that you are clear and consistent. You may need to show that you have heard an objection before you answer it.
