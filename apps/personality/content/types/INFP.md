# INFP

## Summary

You prefer quiet, ideas and possibilities, decisions guided by your values, and open options. You tend to spend a lot of time in your own thoughts, imagining how things could be. You are likely to care strongly about a few people and causes, and to feel the moods of others easily. You usually avoid conflict and competition. Plans, schedules and tidy surroundings tend to matter less to you than staying true to what you care about. You tend to decide late.

## Strengths

You tend to see what matters to a person underneath what they say. You imagine alternatives that others have not considered. You stay with a cause or a person you believe in without needing a reward. You usually give people room to be themselves, and you listen without rushing to fix.

## Blind spots

You may start more things than you finish, because the idea held more interest than the routine. You can take criticism of your work as criticism of you. Practical details and deadlines may slip. You may hold an ideal so high that real people and real results keep falling short of it.

## At work

You tend to want work that means something to you, freedom in how you do it and little conflict. You are likely to prefer working alone or with one or two trusted people. Rigid procedures, close monitoring and work that goes against your values tend to drain you fast. You usually work in bursts of strong interest.

## Under stress

You may withdraw into your thoughts and judge yourself harshly. You might put everything off, or become unusually sharp and critical. Time alone, something creative and one person who listens without giving advice usually help.

## How others may read you

People may see you as gentle, thoughtful and a little hard to pin down. Your quietness can hide how strongly you feel. Some are surprised when you refuse to bend on a matter of principle. You may need to say what you want directly, because hints often go unnoticed.
