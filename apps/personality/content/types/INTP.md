# INTP

## Summary

You prefer quiet, ideas and patterns, decisions made by logic, and open options. You tend to take an idea apart to see whether it holds, and you enjoy that for its own sake. You are likely to question claims, including your own, and to say when an argument has a hole in it. You would usually keep a question open instead of settling it early. Routine, schedules and tidying up tend to hold little interest. You often need long stretches alone to think.

## Strengths

You tend to find the flaw in a line of reasoning and the pattern under a set of facts. You stay with a hard question long after others have accepted a rough answer. You change your mind when the evidence changes. You usually judge an idea on its merits, whoever said it.

## Blind spots

You may keep analyzing after it is time to act. You can correct someone's logic when they wanted support. Follow-through on routine tasks, messages and deadlines may slip. You may explain an idea in more detail than the listener asked for and lose them on the way.

## At work

You tend to want a difficult problem, the freedom to approach it your own way and time to think without interruption. You are likely to prefer a few precise words over a long meeting. Fixed procedures, status reports and decisions made by rank instead of reasons tend to frustrate you. You usually work in uneven bursts.

## Under stress

You may retreat into your head and stop replying to people. You might put off every decision, or become unusually touchy about being misunderstood. Time alone, sleep and a problem small enough to finish usually help.

## How others may read you

People may see you as detached, skeptical and lost in thought. Your questions can sound like disagreement when you are only checking. Those who know you tend to find you open-minded and fair. You may need to tell people you are interested in them, because your attention to ideas is easier to see.
