# ESFP

## Summary

You prefer company and activity, concrete experience, decisions that consider people, and open options. You tend to enjoy what is happening now and to draw others into it. You are likely to be warm, easy to talk to and quick to notice when someone is left out. You learn by doing more than by reading about it. You usually take plans loosely and change them when something better comes up. Long stretches alone or abstract discussion are likely to drain you.

## Strengths

You tend to put people at ease within minutes. You notice practical things others need and help on the spot. You adapt to a change of plan without fuss. You usually lift the mood of a group, and you make ordinary tasks more pleasant to share.

## Blind spots

You may put off anything dull or far away, such as paperwork and saving. You can avoid a serious talk by changing the subject. Promises made in a warm moment may be more than you can keep. You may take criticism personally and for longer than you show.

## At work

You tend to want people around, hands-on tasks and room to move. You are likely to prefer a friendly team over a quiet desk. Long solitary projects, strict routines and cold feedback tend to wear on you. You usually learn a job fastest by being shown once and then trying it.

## Under stress

You may keep busy to avoid the problem, or look for company and distraction. You might start imagining the worst outcome, which is unlike you. Talking with someone you trust, then picking one small next step, usually helps.

## How others may read you

People may see you as cheerful, generous and easy to be around. Some may assume you are never serious. Those close to you tend to know that you feel things strongly and remember kindness. You may need to let people see the worried side as well, so that they can help.
