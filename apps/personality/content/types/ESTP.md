# ESTP

## Summary

You prefer company and activity, concrete facts, decisions made by logic, and open options. You tend to act first and adjust as you go. You notice what is happening in the room and respond to it quickly. You are likely to say what you think plainly and to enjoy some competition. Long plans and theory tend to bore you, and you would sooner try a thing and see. You usually bring energy to a group and get restless when little is happening.

## Strengths

You tend to move while others are still discussing. You read a situation as it is, not as it was supposed to be. You stay practical under pressure and bargain or improvise your way through. You usually speak directly, so people know what you want.

## Blind spots

You may act before you have thought about what follows next month. You can be blunt in a way that costs you goodwill. Routine upkeep and long commitments may get dropped once the exciting part is over. You may take a risk for the sake of the action itself.

## At work

You tend to want variety, quick results and people around you. You are likely to prefer solving a live problem over planning for a possible one. Long meetings, heavy procedure and sitting still tend to wear on you. You usually do your best work when something real is at stake today.

## Under stress

You may push harder, take bigger risks or look for a distraction. You might become impatient and sharp with people who slow you down. Physical activity, and a short concrete list of what to do next, usually help more than a long talk.

## How others may read you

People may see you as confident, fun and direct. Some may read your speed as carelessness and your bluntness as not caring. Those who know you tend to count on you when things go wrong in the moment. You may need to slow down and listen to the end before you answer.
