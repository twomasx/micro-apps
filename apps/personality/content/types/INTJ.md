# INTJ

## Summary

You prefer quiet, ideas and patterns, decisions made by logic, and a clear plan. You tend to build a picture of how something should work, then arrange the steps to get there. You are likely to question a rule until you see its reason. You would usually choose an accurate answer over a comfortable one, and you say so. You tend to work best with long stretches alone. Small talk and loose ends are both likely to wear on you.

## Strengths

You tend to think several steps ahead and to plan for what could go wrong. You judge an idea by whether it holds up, whoever it came from. Once you have a plan you usually carry it out without needing to be pushed. You are comfortable disagreeing with a group when the reasoning points the other way.

## Blind spots

You may settle on a plan in your head and act as if it were agreed before anyone else has heard it. You can undervalue how people feel about a decision that is logically sound. Facts on the ground that do not fit your model may get explained away. You may come across as certain when you are only being direct.

## At work

You tend to want a hard problem, the freedom to solve it your way and few interruptions. You are likely to prefer a written argument over a long meeting. Rules with no stated reason, and work redone for the sake of appearances, tend to frustrate you. You usually hold yourself to a higher standard than anyone has asked for.

## Under stress

You may pull back, work longer hours alone and trust other people's input less. You might grow more critical and more rigid about the plan. Stepping away from the problem and doing something physical usually helps more than thinking harder about it.

## How others may read you

People may read you as distant, sure of yourself and hard to impress. Your criticism of an idea can be heard as criticism of the person. Those who work with you longer tend to find you fair and consistent. You may need to say what you appreciate, because you are likely to voice problems first.
