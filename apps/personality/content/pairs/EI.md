# Extraversion and Introversion, together

## Both E

You both lean toward company and activity. You are likely to agree easily on going out, having people over and talking things through as they happen. Day to day, the risk is a full calendar with little quiet time for the two of you, and two people talking with nobody listening.

## Both I

You both lean toward quiet. You are likely to give each other space without being asked and to be comfortable in silence together. Day to day, the risk is that neither of you raises a problem, and that you see fewer other people than either of you would like.

## One E, one I

One of you gains energy from people and the other from quiet. This tends to show up in how you spend evenings and weekends, how long you stay at gatherings and how soon you talk after a disagreement. One of you may want to talk right away while the other needs time first. Neither need is a comment on the relationship.

## Questions to talk through

- After a hard day, what helps each of you most: company or time alone?
- How many evenings a week with other people feels right to each of you?
- When we disagree, how long does each of us need before talking about it?
- What is a signal we can use at a gathering to say "I am ready to leave"?
