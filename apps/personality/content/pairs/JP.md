# Judging and Perceiving, together

## Both J

You both lean toward plans and order. You are likely to agree on schedules, tidiness and getting decisions made. Day to day, the risk is two firm plans that do not match, and little room for doing something on the spur of the moment.

## Both P

You both lean toward flexibility. You are likely to give each other freedom and to change plans without friction. Day to day, the risk is that decisions, chores and deadlines get put off by both of you until they become urgent.

## One J, one P

One of you likes things settled and the other likes options open. This tends to show up in housework, timekeeping, travel and how early you decide things. One may feel left waiting on a decision, and the other may feel pressed to decide too soon. A different pace is not a lack of care on either side.

## Questions to talk through

- Which decisions need to be made early for one of us to relax, and which can stay open?
- What level of tidiness can we both live with at home?
- How much of a holiday or weekend should be planned ahead?
- Which chores does each of us mind least, and can we split them that way?
