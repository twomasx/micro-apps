# Sensing and Intuition, together

## Both S

You both lean toward facts and known methods. You are likely to agree on practical matters and to talk in concrete terms about what happened and what to do next. Day to day, the risk is staying with what you know when a change would serve you both, and seldom talking about where things are heading in the long run.

## Both N

You both lean toward ideas and possibilities. You are likely to enjoy long talks about plans, meanings and what could be. Day to day, the risk is that practical details such as bills, repairs and bookings belong to nobody, and that plans stay as talk.

## One S, one N

One of you starts from the facts and the other from the idea. This tends to show up in conversation: one wants the specifics, the other wants the point. One may find the other vague, and the other may find the first one stuck on details. Each of you tends to catch what the other misses.

## Questions to talk through

- When we plan something, who looks after the details and who looks after the overall aim? Are we both happy with that?
- What is one thing we keep doing the same way that one of us would like to change?
- When I describe a problem, do you want the facts first or the conclusion first?
- Which practical jobs are falling through the gaps right now?
