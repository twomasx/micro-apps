# Extraversion, together

## Alike

Your scores are close. You are likely to want a similar amount of social time and a similar pace of activity. Day to day, you may agree easily on how many evenings to spend with other people and how long to stay. If you both lean higher, the risk is a full calendar with little time for just the two of you. If you both lean lower, you may see fewer people than either of you would like.

## Different

Your scores are far apart. One of you likely gains energy from people and activity, while the other needs quiet to recover. This tends to show up in evenings, weekends and how long you stay at a gathering. After a disagreement, one of you may want to talk right away while the other needs time first. Neither need is a comment on the relationship, and neither way is better.

## Questions to talk through

- After a hard day, what helps each of us most: company or time alone?
- How many evenings a week with other people feels right to each of us?
- What signal can we use at a gathering to say "I am ready to leave"?
