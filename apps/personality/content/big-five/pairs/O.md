# Openness to experience, together

## Alike

Your scores are close. You are likely to want a similar amount of novelty, so choices about travel, food, books and free time come more easily. Day to day, that means fewer disagreements about whether to try something new. If you both lean higher, the risk is chasing the next new thing and losing time for what you already share. If you both lean lower, the risk is a routine that neither of you thinks to change.

## Different

Your scores are far apart. One of you is likely to want new places, ideas and experiences, while the other prefers what is familiar and known. This tends to show up in holidays, weekend plans and conversation: one of you wants to explore a big idea, the other wants to settle the practical question. Neither way is better. One of you brings fresh ideas and the other brings steadiness.

## Questions to talk through

- How often does each of us want to try something new, and how often do we want the familiar?
- When one of us wants to talk about a big idea, how can the other say whether they have the energy for it?
- What is one new thing we could try together, and one routine we both want to keep?
