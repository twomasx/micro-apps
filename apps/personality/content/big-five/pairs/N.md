# Neuroticism, together

## Alike

Your scores are close. You are likely to react to stress with similar strength, so each of you may find the other's moods easy to understand. If you both lean higher, worry and irritation can build between you, so it helps to agree on how to calm things down. In studies of couples, being alike in personality predicts relationship satisfaction only weakly. How each of you handles negative emotions tends to matter more.

## Different

Your scores are far apart. One of you likely feels worry, irritation and sadness more strongly, while the other stays calmer. The calmer one can help steady things, but may also seem not to take a worry seriously. Neither of you is wrong. In studies of couples, personality similarity overall predicts relationship satisfaction only weakly. How each person handles negative emotions tends to matter more for how satisfied you both feel.

## Questions to talk through

- When one of us is worried or upset, what helps most: reassurance, a plan or just listening?
- What does each of us do when we feel irritated, and how can the other tell?
- How can we calm things down when stress starts to build between us?
