# Agreeableness, together

## Alike

Your scores are close. You are likely to handle disagreements in a similar way and to expect a similar amount of give and take. If you both lean higher, you are likely to be gentle with each other, and the risk is that a hard subject never gets raised. If you both lean lower, you are likely to speak frankly, and the risk is that arguments get sharp and nobody softens first.

## Different

Your scores are far apart. One of you is likely to give way to keep the peace and to notice feelings first, while the other is more likely to speak bluntly and stand firm. This tends to show up in arguments and in dealing with family, friends or a landlord. Frankness may hurt the gentler one, and tact may look evasive to the blunter one. Neither way is better.

## Questions to talk through

- When we disagree, does each of us feel free to say what we really think?
- How does each of us prefer to hear criticism?
- Is there something one of us has been agreeing to only to keep the peace?
