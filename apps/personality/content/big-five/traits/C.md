# Conscientiousness

## What this trait measures

This trait measures how much you organize your life, keep to plans and follow through on what you start. It has two parts: Industriousness, which is about getting work done, and Orderliness, which is about order and routine.

## Higher

Your answers leaned toward the higher end. You are likely to plan ahead, keep your things and your time in order, and finish what you start. Loose ends and missed deadlines tend to bother you, and you may hold yourself to a stricter standard than anyone asked for.

## Middle

Your answers landed near the middle. You probably plan when it matters and let things slide when it does not. Some parts of your life may be well ordered while others stay loose. How much you follow through likely depends on how much the task means to you.

## Lower

Your answers leaned toward the lower end. You are likely to prefer keeping things open and flexible over fixed plans and routines. You tend to work in bursts when something catches your interest, and a messy desk or a loose schedule is unlikely to bother you much.

## Higher in depth

In everyday life, you are likely to keep lists, use a calendar and know where your things are. You tend to deal with a chore soon rather than let it build up. Plans that change at the last minute are likely to unsettle you.

At work, you tend to start on time, keep a steady effort and check your work before you hand it in. People are likely to trust you with tasks that have deadlines. You may find it hard to stop before something is complete, or to accept "good enough".

With other people, you are likely to keep your promises and to expect the same in return. Lateness and broken plans can annoy you. People who live more loosely may see you as rigid, so it can help to say plainly which plans matter to you and which can bend.

## Middle in depth

In everyday life, you probably keep order where it counts to you, such as bills or appointments, and let other areas go. You may make a plan, follow part of it and adjust the rest as you go. A messy room can sit for a while before it bothers you.

At work, you are likely to meet the deadlines that matter and to work steadily on tasks you care about. You may put off dull tasks until they become urgent. You can usually work with a strict process or without one.

With other people, you are likely to be reliable about what you have promised and relaxed about small plans. You may find that you are the organized one with some friends and the easygoing one with others. It can help to tell people which of your plans are firm and which are loose.

## Lower in depth

In everyday life, you are likely to decide things as they come rather than plan far ahead. Your space may be cluttered without it getting in your way. You tend to change course easily when something better turns up.

At work, you are likely to work best on tasks that hold your interest, often in bursts close to a deadline. Strict procedures, detailed checklists and long stretches of routine may wear on you. You tend to be comfortable when plans shift at the last minute.

With other people, you are likely to be easygoing about lateness, mess and changed plans, in yourself and in others. People who rely on fixed plans may read this as not caring. Saying clearly which commitments you will hold to can help them trust the flexible ones.
