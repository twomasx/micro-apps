# Enthusiasm

## What this aspect measures

This aspect measures how warm and outgoing you are, how easily you make friends and how often you feel and show positive emotions such as joy and excitement.

## Higher

Your answers leaned toward the higher end. You are likely to make friends easily, show your feelings openly and feel cheerful or excited often. You tend to warm to new people quickly and to enjoy being around others. People can usually tell how you feel.

## Lower

Your answers leaned toward the lower end. You are likely to take time to warm up to new people and to keep your feelings to yourself. Your good moods tend to be quiet rather than excited. You may have a few close friends and feel no need for more.
