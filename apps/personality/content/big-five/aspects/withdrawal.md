# Withdrawal

## What this aspect measures

This aspect measures how much you worry, doubt yourself, feel threatened or overwhelmed, and feel discouraged or low.

## Higher

Your answers leaned toward the higher end. You are likely to worry about what might go wrong, doubt your choices and feel overwhelmed when demands pile up. Setbacks may leave you discouraged for a while. This describes how you tend to feel. It is not a diagnosis.

## Lower

Your answers leaned toward the lower end. You are likely to feel at ease with yourself and to stay hopeful when things go wrong. Worry and self-doubt tend not to take hold, and you usually feel able to cope with what comes. Setbacks are likely to pass without leaving you discouraged.
