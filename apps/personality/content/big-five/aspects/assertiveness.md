# Assertiveness

## What this aspect measures

This aspect measures how much you take charge, speak up, try to influence others and act first in a group.

## Higher

Your answers leaned toward the higher end. You are likely to say what you think in a group, take the lead when nobody else does and try to persuade people. You tend to act first and to be comfortable when others follow your direction.

## Lower

Your answers leaned toward the lower end. You are likely to let others take the lead and to share your view when asked rather than push it forward. You tend to prefer influencing quietly, or not at all, over directing a group. You may hold back in a meeting even when you have something to say.
