# Politeness

## What this aspect measures

This aspect measures how much you show respect, hold back from pushing your own way and avoid conflict and taking advantage of others.

## Higher

Your answers leaned toward the higher end. You are likely to respect rules and other people's wishes, avoid arguments and hold back from pushing your own interests. You tend to feel uneasy using people to get what you want. You may give way to keep the peace.

## Lower

Your answers leaned toward the lower end. You are likely to challenge people, including those in charge, and to argue for what you want. You tend to say blunt things when you think they need saying, and you may enjoy a debate. Manners matter less to you than saying what you think.
