# Industriousness

## What this aspect measures

This aspect measures how readily you get started on tasks, keep your focus and see them through to the end.

## Higher

Your answers leaned toward the higher end. You are likely to start tasks without much delay, keep working when they get dull and finish what you begin. You tend to set goals and push toward them steadily. Wasted time may bother you more than hard work does.

## Lower

Your answers leaned toward the lower end. You are likely to put off tasks that do not interest you and to work in bursts rather than at a steady pace. Your focus may drift, and you may leave some things half done when something new comes along. You tend to work hardest on what you care about.
