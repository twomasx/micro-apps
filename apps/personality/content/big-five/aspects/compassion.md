# Compassion

## What this aspect measures

This aspect measures how much you care about other people's feelings and needs, and how much you want to help.

## Higher

Your answers leaned toward the higher end. You are likely to notice when someone is upset, feel their distress and want to help. You tend to make time for people's problems and to weigh their feelings when you decide something. Other people's hardship can stay with you.

## Lower

Your answers leaned toward the lower end. You are likely to keep some emotional distance from other people's problems. You tend to weigh practical facts over feelings when you decide something, and you may expect people to handle their own troubles. This does not mean you are unkind. Feelings are just not your first focus.
