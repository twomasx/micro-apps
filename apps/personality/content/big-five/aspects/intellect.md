# Intellect

## What this aspect measures

This aspect measures how much you enjoy and seek out ideas, such as reading, abstract thinking and puzzles, not how smart you are.

## Higher

Your answers leaned toward the higher end. You are likely to enjoy thinking about abstract ideas, to read for the pleasure of learning and to take on hard questions for their own sake. A problem without an obvious answer is more likely to draw you in than to put you off.

## Lower

Your answers leaned toward the lower end. You are likely to prefer concrete, practical matters over abstract ideas, and to read or study for a clear purpose rather than for its own sake. Long debates about theory may hold little interest for you. You tend to want to know what an idea is for.
