# Openness

## What this aspect measures

This aspect measures how much you respond to beauty, art, music, nature and imagination, and how much attention you give to your inner experience.

## Higher

Your answers leaned toward the higher end. You are likely to be moved by music, art or a view, to daydream and to notice your own feelings and impressions in detail. You may seek out new sights, sounds and experiences for their own sake.

## Lower

Your answers leaned toward the lower end. You are likely to focus on the world as it is rather than on daydreams or impressions. Art and music may be things you enjoy casually rather than things that move you deeply. You tend to stay grounded in practical, present concerns.
