# Orderliness

## What this aspect measures

This aspect measures how much you like order, routine, tidiness, attention to detail and following rules.

## Higher

Your answers leaned toward the higher end. You are likely to keep your things in their place, follow a routine and notice small details. You tend to like clear rules and a set way of doing things. Mess and clutter may bother you until you put them right.

## Lower

Your answers leaned toward the lower end. You are likely to be comfortable with some mess and to change your routine as you go. Details and rules may matter less to you than the overall result. You tend to prefer a loose structure that leaves room to improvise.
