# Volatility

## What this aspect measures

This aspect measures how easily you get angry or irritated, how quickly your mood changes and how often you lose your composure.

## Higher

Your answers leaned toward the higher end. You are likely to feel irritation or anger quickly and strongly, and your mood can change fast. Small frustrations may set you off, and you may say things in the moment that you later regret. Your feelings usually show.

## Lower

Your answers leaned toward the lower end. You are likely to stay even-tempered and slow to anger. Your mood tends to stay steady through the day, and you usually keep your composure when something goes wrong. Irritation tends to pass quickly without being said out loud.
