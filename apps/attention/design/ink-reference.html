Ink

Sumi ink on cool paper with a red seal. While you focus, a brush slowly draws an enso, the Zen circle. Hold the stretch and it stays in ink. Drift and it fades to gray. Each session leaves a row of circles, one per stretch.

Demo timing: stretches start at 6 seconds and change by 25% each answer. The real program starts near 15 seconds, adapts more gently, and runs about 10 to 20 minutes a day.
