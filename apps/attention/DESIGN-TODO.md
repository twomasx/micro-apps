# Attention: open decisions for Roger

Decisions built the simplest way for now that need your call. What has been decided, and why, is in `DESIGN-NOTES.md`.

## Open decisions

1. **The sounds.** Try them at `/attention/sounds` (development only) with the phone speaker and with headphones: the tok, the bell, the tick, and a 10 s stretch that plays all three in order. Say if the tok is too quiet on the speaker, the bell too bright, or the tick too easy or too hard to hear.
2. **Default volume** is 80%.
3. **Tick probes are on by default** for new users. They can be switched off in settings.
